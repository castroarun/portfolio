## What is CMMI Level 4?

**CMMI (Capability Maturity Model Integration)** is an industry-standard quality assurance control framework for governance and processing of a team or unit's workflow and deliverables structure.

### CMMI Levels
| Level | Name | Meaning |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Initial | Unpredictable, reactive |
| 2 | Managed | Project-level processes |
| 3 | Defined | Organization-level processes |
| **4** | **Quantitatively Managed** | **Processes measured & controlled with statistical metrics** |
| 5 | Optimizing | Continuous improvement |

### Why Level 4 Matters
- Processes are **established**
- Processes are **followed**
- Processes are **controlled with statistical metrics**
- **No other projects in the region had achieved this level**

## The Challenge
Implementing rigorous quality processes while:
- Delivering on tight timelines
- Managing team resistance to "overhead" processes
- Maintaining productivity while adding governance
- Achieving statistical control of defect rates

## What I Did
- Took on **QA Lead role additionally** (beyond Project Manager responsibilities)
- Established quality processes and metrics tracking

- Led the team through the assessment process
