## Philosophy & Approach

### Morning Training
Train before work - sets the tone for the entire day.

### Why It Works

| Benefit | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Endorphin Release** | Natural stress buster |
| **Dopamine Boost** | Improved mood throughout the day |
| **Mental Clarity** | Sharper thinking for work challenges |
| **Consistency** | Builds discipline that transfers to all life areas |
| **Discipline** | Morning commitment creates structured mindset |

### Core Belief
> "Strength training in the morning before work is an excellent stress buster that releases endorphins and dopamine, boosting mood and mental clarity for the rest of the day. It imparts consistency and discipline in other aspects of life including work."
