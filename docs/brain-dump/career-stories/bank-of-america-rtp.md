## What is RTP (Real-Time Payments)?

> "All payments in the real world are instant - to be instantly received, acknowledged, validated, processed and settled in no time. To the customer, this end loop must be instant."

### The RTP Promise
| Step | Must Be |
|------|---------|
| Received | Instant |
| Acknowledged | Instant |
| Validated | Instant |
| Processed | Instant |
| Settled | Instant |
| **Customer Experience** | **Seamless, immediate** |

## APAC Region - What Makes It Unique

### Cultural & Regulatory Diversity
> "APAC regions have culturally different banks including banks from the US/global banks amidst Chinese, Japanese, Singaporean and Indonesian - different clearing houses. Just have to be looked at as separate entities, unlearn and approach an implementation."

### The Challenge
| Country/Region | Consideration |
|----------------|---------------|
| US/Global Banks | Global standards, familiar processes |
| Chinese Banks | Different regulations, clearing houses |
| Japanese Banks | Unique banking culture and systems |
| Singaporean Banks | MAS regulations, advanced fintech |
| Indonesian Banks | OJK regulations, emerging market dynamics |

### Required Mindset
- **Treat each as separate entity**
- **Unlearn** previous assumptions
- **Approach fresh** for each implementation
- **Different clearing houses** = different integration patterns

## Current Win: NFR Automation

### The Problem
NFR (Non-Functional Requirements) tasks were:
- Manually curated
- Hard to track
- Lacked traceability
- Disconnected from deliverables

### My Solution
Automated the entire NFR lifecycle:

| Automated Step | Benefit |
|----------------|---------|
| **Curation** | Systematic identification of NFRs |
| **Creation** | Standardized task creation |
| **Tracking** | Real-time visibility into progress |
| **Traceability** | Requirements linked to deliverables |
| **End-to-End Driving** | Requirements → Deliverables ownership |

### Impact
- Driving NFR requirements to deliverables end-to-end
- Established traceability across the lifecycle
- Systematic approach replacing ad-hoc processes
